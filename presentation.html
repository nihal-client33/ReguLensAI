Slide 01
01 • Industry Context & Problem Statement

The Banking Regulatory Compliance Crisis

Banks navigate hundreds of circulars annually across fractured spreadsheets, inviting severe penalties.

📑
Regulatory Velocity & Volume
Central regulators (RBI, Basel Committee, FATF, DPDP, DORA) issue hundreds of amendments annually. Critical clauses buried inside 80-page PDFs are missed or improperly classified.
🧩
Disconnected Control Frameworks
Internal policies, SOPs, and IT controls remain isolated in department silos. Changes in regulatory cadences (e.g. Annual to Quarterly) are not reflected in automated core banking schedulers.
⚖️
Zero Audit Traceability & Penalties
Auditors cannot verify why a specific control was deemed sufficient for a regulatory requirement. Failure to evidence compliance exposes banks to severe statutory fines under Banking Regulation Act Section 47A.
The ReguLens AI Solution
An agentic AI nervous system that continuously reads regulations, extracts obligations, maps controls, audits evidence, and synthesizes remediation.

03 • Multi-Agent Cognitive System

11 Specialized Autonomous AI Agents

A coordinated agent swarm with deterministic reasoning, confidence metrics, and role separation.

AGENT 01
Regulatory Intelligence Agent
Reads legal circulars, extracts metadata, tracks versions, and detects word-level diffs.
AGENT 02
Obligation Extraction Agent
Decomposes complex paragraphs into atomic obligations with cadence, risk, and owner.
AGENT 03
Applicability Agent
Determines scope across retail, corporate, treasury, and cross-border banking entities.
AGENT 04
Control Framework Agent
Ingests internal SOPs, controls, owners, procedures, and testing frequencies into inventory.
AGENT 05
Control Mapping Agent
Maps obligations to controls using semantic cosine matching and confidence scoring (0-100%).
AGENT 06
Evidence Assessment Agent
Audits test reports, logs, and CSV samples for sufficiency, expiration, and contradictions.
AGENT 07
Control Effectiveness Agent
Evaluates design vs operating effectiveness based on historical test executions and drift.
AGENT 08
Gap Analysis Agent
Identifies missing, weak, or cadence-mismatched controls; generates unique GAP IDs.
AGENT 09
Risk Prioritization Agent
Calculates explainable risk scores factoring regulatory severity, customer harm, and penalties.
AGENT 10
Remediation Agent
Formulates step-by-step action plans, policy adjustments, and owner milestone deadlines.
AGENT 11
Regulatory Impact Agent
Simulates the cascade blast radius of circular revisions across all downstream bank policies.
